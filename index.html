Reading together at home

1Start at the right level

Explorers is for first number skills. Geniuses adds and subtracts up to four digits. Ninjas practise times tables. Masters learn division. Wizards work with fractions, decimals, integers and powers.

2Little and often

Ten minutes a day works better than one long session. Let your child solve a page aloud, then check together.

3Accuracy before speed

Each book ends with a Challenge Time page. Time it only once your child gets the answers right without counting on fingers.
